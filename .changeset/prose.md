Prose: a new component for Markdown, CMS and rich-text content. Inside it, each block is spaced from the one before (more before a heading, none before the first), lists get their markers and logical indents back, and quotes, code, tables, rules and images are styled. A wide table or code block scrolls in its own box, and a visited link takes the new `link-visited` color (4.5:1 on `surface` and `surface-muted` in all six themes), with its utility classes.
