Table: a sticky header lifts once rows scroll under it (KW16). A soft shadow (`--table-head-shadow-scrolled`, default `shadow-sm`) fades in over the first header height of the frame's scroll, tied to the scroll so nothing shows at the top and it never runs on its own, as the sticky Navbar does. Where scroll-driven animations aren't supported, the header stays flat.
