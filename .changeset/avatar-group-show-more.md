- Added: AvatarGroup `showMore` makes "+N" a button that opens a popover listing the people not shown, and `onMore` makes it a button that calls your handler. Before, "+N" couldn't be reached by keyboard or touch, so those users could never see who was behind it.
- Added: the `--avatar-group-more-hover-bg` and `--avatar-group-focus-ring` component tokens, for the "+N" button's hover background and focus ring.
