- Added: `CheckboxGroup`, which holds a list of checked values (`value`, `defaultValue`, `onValueChange`) for a set of Checkboxes. It names them and passes `size`, `invalid`, `disabled` and `required` down. Here `required` means at least one box. Add `<Checkbox selectAll>` to check or clear the rest: it's checked when all are, and indeterminate when some are.
