Added: TreeView, nested items that open and close, such as folders and files. It follows the WAI-ARIA tree pattern: one Tab stop, the arrow keys, Home and End, `*`, and typeahead. `selectionMode` is 'single', 'multiple' (with Shift ranges and Ctrl+A) or 'none'. `value` and `expanded` can be controlled or uncontrolled, closed items' children aren't in the page, and every value has its own token.
