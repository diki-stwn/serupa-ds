- Added: Slider `marks`, with ticks on the track (`true` for every step) and optional labels under them. Also `showValue`, which shows the formatted value over the thumb while it's focused, hovered or dragged. Both are visual only, and come with the tokens `--slider-mark`, `--slider-mark-label`, `--slider-value-bg` and `--slider-value-text`.
