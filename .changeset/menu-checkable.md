- Added: `MenuCheckboxItem` (role `menuitemcheckbox`) for on/off settings in a Menu, and `MenuRadioGroup` with `MenuRadioItem` (role `menuitemradio`) for one of a set, each with `aria-checked` and a check before the label. You hold the state (`checked` with `onCheckedChange`, `value` with `onValueChange`). The arrow keys, Home, End and typeahead reach them like any item.
