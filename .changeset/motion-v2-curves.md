Added: smoother motion curves. New easings in three strengths, `ease-out-cubic`, `-quart` and `-expo`, `ease-in-cubic`, `-quart` and `-expo`, and `ease-in-out-cubic`, `-quart` and `-expo`; `ease-out-back`, which passes the end by about 4% and comes back; and two real springs written as CSS `linear()`, `ease-spring-soft` and `ease-spring-snappy`, generated by `tools/tokens/spring.mjs`. Two new easing roles use them: `ease-pop` for something small popping into place and `ease-spring` for a press let go springing back. Each has a utility class (`.ease-out-expo`, `.ease-spring` and the rest).

Added: stagger. `motion-stagger` (50ms, the new `delay-50`) is the gap between items of a group arriving one after another and `motion-stagger-limit` (300ms) the longest any item waits. The `stagger` utility class gives each child of its element that delay from its place, as `animation-delay` and as `--dk-stagger-delay` for your own transitions, and the new `animate-enter` class fades content in as it rises 8px into place, so `<ul class="stagger">` with `animate-enter` items lands them one after another. Under reduced motion `animate-enter` only fades and the stagger delays are dropped.

Changed: the easing roles take the smoother curves, so every component's motion feels more polished with the same durations. `ease-enter` is now `ease-out-quart` (was `ease-out`), `ease-exit` `ease-in-cubic` (was `ease-in`), `ease-move` `ease-in-out-cubic` (was `ease-in-out`) and `ease-settle` `ease-out-expo` (was `ease-out`). Nothing to change in your code; set the role back on `:root` (for example `--ease-enter: var(--ease-out)`) to keep the old feel.
