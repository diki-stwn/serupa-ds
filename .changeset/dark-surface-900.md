- **Breaking.** Changed: the three dark themes are lighter. The page (`surface`) is `zinc-900` instead of the near-black `zinc-950`, and every layer above it moves up one step so they stay apart: `surface-muted`, `surface-raised` and `surface-overlay` are `zinc-800` (were `zinc-900`); `border` and `border-raised` are `zinc-700` (were `zinc-800`); `border-strong` and `border-overlay` are `zinc-600` (were `zinc-700`); `secondary` is `zinc-700` (was `zinc-800`) and `secondary-hover` is `zinc-600` (was `zinc-700`). `field`, `highlight`, `pressed`, text, brand and status colors keep their values, and the light themes don't change. `highlight` now shares `secondary`'s `zinc-700` in dark, so a `primary` bar on it keeps 3:1 in the violet brand. Text keeps WCAG 2.2 AA (and the stronger `data-contrast="more"` pairs) on every surface, and `field-border` keeps 3:1 on fields, pages and cards. `theme.js` writes `#18181b` (was `#09090b`) to `<meta name="theme-color">` before the stylesheets arrive. **Migration:** nothing to do if you use the tokens; your app gets the lighter dark mode. Check anything you colored with raw `zinc-950`, `zinc-900` or `zinc-800` next to Serupa surfaces in dark, since those now sit one step off: replace them with the token (`surface`, `surface-raised`, `border`) or move them up a step. A custom dark `theme-color` or a hardcoded `#09090b` page background should become `#18181b`. To keep the old near-black look, set `--surface: var(--zinc-950)` and the other tokens above back to their old steps in your dark theme.
