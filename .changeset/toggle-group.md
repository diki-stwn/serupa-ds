Added: ToggleGroup, a set of ToggleButtons that share one value. With `type="single"` (the default) one is on at a time, and `required` keeps one on. With `"multiple"` any number can be on. `value`, `defaultValue` and `onValueChange` hold the values of the buttons that are on. `size`, `variant` and `disabled` reach every button that doesn't set its own. It looks like a ButtonGroup and has its `attached` and `orientation` props.
